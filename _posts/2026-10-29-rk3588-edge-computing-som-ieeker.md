## Common Industrial Use Cases
### Machine Vision & Inspection
The RK3588’s NPU chews through object detection, classification, and other vision models right on the spot. Factories rely on it for checking products for defects, scanning barcodes automatically, and keeping an eye on operations in real time.

### Smart Edge Gateway
This setup grabs data from sensors, PLCs, and cameras, does the pre-processing up front, and only sends what’s really needed to the cloud. That means less bandwidth hogging and faster responses.

### Offline AI Voice & Multimedia
With beefy video chops, this module is perfect for offline voice assistants, multi-screen displays, and devices that need to play back media locally—no internet required.

### Is the RK3588 good for low-power projects?
Nope—the RK3588 aims for performance. If you want something that barely sips electricity, look at the RK3566 or RK3568 chips instead.

### Can the SOM handle wild temperature swings?
